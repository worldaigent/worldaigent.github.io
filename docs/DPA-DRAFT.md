## 1. Definitions

"GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679. "Personal data", "processing",
"controller", "processor", "sub-processor", "data subject" and "supervisory
authority" carry the meanings given in the GDPR. "Services" means the services
described in your client agreement.

## 2. Roles

You are the controller and we are the processor in respect of personal data we
process to provide the Services. Each party complies with its own obligations
under applicable data protection law.

You are responsible for the lawfulness of the personal data you provide to us
and of the instructions you give, including having a lawful basis for
publishing content and for any personal data contained in material you submit.

We act as an independent controller only in respect of our own business
records, such as your account and billing details, and website enquiries. That
processing is governed by our Privacy Policy, not by this Agreement.

## 3. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose

**Subject matter.** Processing of personal data necessary to provide the
Services.

**Duration.** The term of your client agreement, plus the deletion periods in
section 10.

**Nature and purpose.** Generating, scheduling and publishing social media
content on your behalf to the accounts you connect; retrieving and analysing
the performance of content on those accounts; producing reports; sending email
you ask us to send; and processing source material you submit so that it can
become content.

The categories of data subject and types of personal data are in **Annex 1**.

## 4. Instructions

We process personal data only on your documented instructions, including the
instructions you give through the Service itself, unless required otherwise by
Union or Member State law. Where we are so required, we will inform you before
processing unless that law prohibits it on important grounds of public
interest.

We will inform you if, in our opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or
other applicable data protection law.

## 5. Confidentiality

appropriate duty of confidentiality and are granted access only where necessary

## 9. Assistance

**Data subject rights.** Taking into account the nature of the processing, we
assist you by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as
possible, in fulfilling your obligation to respond to requests to exercise data
subject rights. If a data subject contacts us directly about data we process on
your behalf, we will forward the request to you and will not respond
substantively ourselves.

**Other obligations.** We assist you in ensuring compliance with Articles 32 to
36, taking into account the nature of processing and the information available
to us.

**Personal data breach.** We notify you **without undue delay** after becoming
aware of a personal data breach affecting personal data processed on your
behalf, and provide the information you reasonably need to meet your own
notification obligations, including the nature of the breach, the categories
and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely
consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

## 10. Deletion and return

On termination of the Services, and at your choice:

- access and refresh tokens for connected accounts are deleted **immediately**
  as part of the termination;
- other personal data processed on your behalf is deleted within **90 days**,
  unless you request export before that period, in which case we return it in a
  commonly used format and then delete it;
- we retain personal data only where required by Union or Member State law, in
  which case we continue to protect it under this Agreement.

Suspension is not termination. Where the Services are paused, for example for
non-payment, connected accounts remain connected and their tokens are retained
so that the Services can resume without you authorising us again.

Content already published to a third-party platform at your instruction remains
on that platform under your control and is not within our power to delete.

## 11. Audit

# Annex 3: Technical and organisational measures

**Encryption.** All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Data
at rest is encrypted using AES-256.

**Access control.** Access to production systems is restricted to authorised
personnel using role-based access controls. Credentials and third-party access
tokens are held in a dedicated secret management service and never written to
source code, databases, or logs.

**Isolation.** Each client is processed in a dedicated namespace. No client's
workflow can observe or affect another's. Data obtained from a connected
account is used only for the client who authorised that connection and is never
combined with another client's data.

**Traceability.** Every workflow execution is recorded, so any action taken on
your account can be traced to the instruction that caused it.

**AI model processing.** We do not use your content, or data obtained from your
connected accounts, to train AI models. We select providers and service tiers
under which submitted content is not used for the provider's own training, and
requests are routed with provider data collection denied.
